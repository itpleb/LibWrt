Feature / 本期特稿

The machine has been running for three days without a single interruption. Traffic moves through the br-lan bridge in a steady, unhurried rhythm.

Six interfaces are recorded in this issue. Two of them carry the whole household; the rest wait, quietly, for a packet that may never arrive. It is a portrait of infrastructure at rest.

Rather than decorate the dashboard, we chose to typeset it — with a masthead, a folio, and hairline rules. The data is the story; the design merely sets it in a readable column.
