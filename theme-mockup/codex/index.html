Incipit liber systematis de router

A manuscript describing the estate of the router: its interfaces, its burdens, its silent servants.

FOLIO MMXXVI · LibWrt · qualcommax/ipq60xx

The router has labored three days without ceasing. Six interfaces are bound within this codex: two bear the traffic of the household, four wait in silence for packets that may never arrive. The traffic flows through br-lan as ink flows through a quill—steady, patient, without haste.

Interfaces

Two interfaces bear the greater burden: eth0 faces the world beyond the walls, br-lan shelters the household within. Four wireless interfaces—two at 2.4GHz, two at 5GHz—wait like scribes awaiting dictation. Their traffic is recorded in the folios that follow.

Rather than illuminate the dashboard with glowing gauges and shouting gauges, we chose to transcribe it—as a scribe would: with a masthead, a folio, and hairline rules. The data is the text; the design merely binds it.

The Machine at Rest

Three days without ceasing. CPU 23%, Memory 61%, fourteen devices connected. The machine rests, yet it is never idle. Every packet is a stroke of the quill; every log entry, a marginal note.

Explicit liber systematis. Deo gratias.
